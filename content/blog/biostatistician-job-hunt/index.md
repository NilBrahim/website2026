I spent the last 2 months looking for a biostatistician position, and I ended up applying for jobs in Yerevan.

During my job search, I started noticing something. It seemed to me that the more developed a country was, the more biostatistics job opportunities there were. (Obviously, this is NOT a statistically valid conclusion 😁So just take it as an observation I had, not as a fact!)

So I decided to apply for jobs in Yerevan, a city I’ve been to a few times and really loved! I’ll write more about my feelings about the city soon.

I applied for 2 positions in Yerevan and got offers for both of them. The whole process took about 2 months, and today I finally accepted an offer from Gurus CRO.

I actually practiced a lot for the technical interviews. ChatGPT helped me a lot. I put it in strict interviewer mode and asked it to grill me. It honestly became annoying sometimes, but it really helped me figure out where my weaknesses were.

I took lots of notes while preparing, and I’ve put them here in the repository. Last year, I also read and practiced many chapters from Nicholas P. Jewell’s *Statistics for Epidemiology*, which was really helpful.

One thing I found really interesting was what happened after I applied.

For both opportunities, I applied first. The recruiters viewed my LinkedIn… and then nothing.

So I messaged them, sent them my CV, and briefly told them how interested I was in the position.

And then I got the first interview after messaging them!

So, one lesson I learned from this experience:

**Don’t give up too soon.**
